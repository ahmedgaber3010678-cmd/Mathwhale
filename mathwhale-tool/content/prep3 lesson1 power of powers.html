Learn 1 Power of a Power Law:
For any real number a (a≠0) and any natural numbers m and n, the following rule holds:
(am)n = am×n
That is:
When raising a power to another power, multiply the exponents while keeping the base the same.
3 Factors
(52)3 = 52×52×52
    = 52+2+2 = 56
That is:
(52)3 = 52×3 = 56

Remember that:
For any two non-zero real numbers a, b where: a≠0, b≠0, and any integers m, n:

1am × an = am+n
2am ÷ an = am−n
3(a×b)m = am × bm
4(ab)m = ambm
5a−n = 1an
6a0 = 1

Learn 2 The law of the power of a product of two powers, and the power of a quotient of two powers:
Product of Powers Rule
For any real numbers a and b, and any natural numbers m, n, and k:
(am × bn)k = amk × bnk
Example:
(23×3)4 = 23×4×31×4 = 212×34
Quotient of Powers Rule
For any real numbers a and b (b≠0), and any natural numbers m, n, and k:
ambnk = amkbnk
Example:
(2372)5 = 23×572×5 = 215710

These rules make it easier to work with powers in multiplication and division!

Important Notes:
1(am + bn)k ≠ amk + bnk
2(am − bn)k ≠ amk − bnk
